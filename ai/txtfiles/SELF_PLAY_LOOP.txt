MTG AI PLAYER
IMPROVING THE POLICY: THE SELF-PLAY LOOP
Written 2026-09-03. Companion to ai/HANDOFF.md.

======================================================================
0. THE SHORT ANSWER
======================================================================

Do NOT record the policy playing against itself.

Record POLICY + SEARCH on both sides, then train the policy on those
games. The search is the teacher; the bare policy is the student.

Everything below is why, and how.

======================================================================
1. WHY POLICY vs POLICY DOES NOTHING
======================================================================

Training a model on its own choices means the labels ARE what the model
already outputs. Three things follow:

  - Agreement climbs toward 100%, because it is agreeing with itself.
  - The gradient goes to nearly zero on every decision it already gets
    right, which is most of them.
  - The win rate does not move.

No new information enters the system. 500 games and an hour of training
buys nothing but sharper versions of the opinions already held.

A learning loop needs a teacher STRONGER than the student. The whole
trick is finding one that is free.

======================================================================
2. WHAT TO RECORD INSTEAD: THE GAP
======================================================================

Measured 2026-09-03, 100 games vs Greedy, seats alternated, +/-5%:

    policy alone           23%     the student, one forward pass
    policy + search (d2)   53%     the teacher, same network + minimax
                           ----
                           30 points of gap

The search plays better than the network inside it because it actually
looks at what happens next. That 30-point gap is the improvement signal.

The loop:

    record the SEARCH's decisions
         |
         v
    train the network to reproduce them in one forward pass
         |
         v
    the network inherits search strength for free
         |
         v
    put search on top of the BETTER network
         |
         v
    stronger still  ->  record again

Same shape as AlphaZero's policy improvement step. Note that everything
before this point was capped at Greedy's strength by construction; this
is the first loop that is not.

======================================================================
3. ONE TURN OF THE CRANK, STEP BY STEP
======================================================================

STEP 1 - Get the previous generation's games out of the way.
---------------------------------------------------------------
data.py discovers files with rglob("*.jsonl") - it RECURSES. Moving the
old set into ai/training/datasets/gen0_greedy/ does NOT hide it from a
bare `python -m mtgai.policy`. You would silently be back to training on
two teachers that disagree, which is the exact problem the single-teacher
retrain was meant to fix.

Two ways out, pick one and stick to it:

  a) Always pass an explicit glob. Never run `python -m mtgai.policy`
     with no arguments again.
  b) Keep archived generations in ai/training/archive/ (gitignored),
     outside the datasets/ tree, so a bare run means "the current
     generation" and nothing else.

STEP 2 - Serve the current checkpoint and confirm it.
---------------------------------------------------------------
    cd ai/python && source .venv/bin/activate
    python -m mtgai.serve --policy-checkpoint checkpoints/policy_net.pt
    curl -s localhost:8787/health
        -> "policy": true, "feature_count": 1295

A stopped or half-loaded server does not fail. It plays as Greedy and
still reports a win rate. Check, every time.

STEP 3 - Record 500 games, not 1000.
---------------------------------------------------------------
Training screen -> both agents "Policy + Search AI" -> tick Record -> Run.

There is a network round trip per decision plus a depth-2 search, so this
is much slower per game than Greedy was. Run 500, look at the elapsed
time, then decide whether 1000 is worth it.

The Training screen health-gates policysearch as of commit afcd3ac. Before
that it only gated "policy" and "valuenet", so a dead server started the
series anyway and recorded 500 games of Greedy under the policy's name.

STEP 4 - Retrain on ONLY the new set, into its own folder.
---------------------------------------------------------------
    python -m mtgai.policy ../training/datasets/games_<new-stamp>*.jsonl \
      --max-per-game 120 --epochs 60 --out-dir checkpoints/gen1

Its target is now an agent that searched, so the policy inherits search
strength in one forward pass.

STEP 5 - Serve gen1, then measure.
---------------------------------------------------------------
    python -m mtgai.serve --policy-checkpoint checkpoints/gen1/policy_net.pt
    godot --headless -s tests/sweep_blend.gd -- 100

The number that matters is the `policy` row: it should land well above
23%, because its teacher searched. Then `policy+search` on top of it
should beat 53%.

The sweep writes its rows into data/ai_stats.json, so the AI Stats screen
shows the new numbers with no further work.

STEP 6 - Repeat from step 1.
---------------------------------------------------------------

======================================================================
4. TRAPS
======================================================================

4.1 THE AGENTS ARE COMPLETELY DETERMINISTIC
-------------------------------------------
There is no temperature, no noise, no epsilon anywhere in
policy_agent.gd, policy_search_agent.gd or greedy_agent.gd. Given the
same position they always play the same move, so the ONLY source of
variety in self-play is the shuffle. The network keeps seeing its own
favourite lines and never sees the alternatives.

This is why AlphaZero adds Dirichlet noise at the root and samples early
moves instead of taking the argmax.

If a cycle stops improving, THIS is the first thing to add - not more
games. Sample the root move from the top-K for the first few turns, in
recording mode only, so measurement stays deterministic.

4.2 MEASURE AGAINST GREEDY, NEVER THE PREVIOUS GENERATION
----------------------------------------------------------
Greedy is a fixed yardstick. "gen2 beats gen1" can keep reading as
progress while both drift downward in absolute terms. Keep the control in
every sweep.

4.3 THE CONTROL IS CURRENTLY READING 56%
-----------------------------------------
Greedy self-play should sit on 50%. It read 56% on 2026-09-03. At +/-5%
standard error that is ~1.2 sigma, so probably noise - but it is the
CONTROL, and every other row on the sweep inherits any tilt it has. Run
`sweep_blend.gd -- 300` once and check it settles before trusting
generation-to-generation comparisons.

4.4 A CHECKPOINT AND ai/training/vocabulary.json TRAVEL TOGETHER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Card ids are assigned in first-encountered order. The Godot StateEncoder
reads vocabulary.json at startup; the model expects the ordering baked
into its own spec. If they disagree, every card id is wrong and nothing
says so - /health compares feature_count only, not the vocabulary.

Both are in git, so commit them in the same commit and they cannot drift
across machines. Serve one checkpoint at a time. An opponent pool of
several generations in one process is NOT safe until /health also checks
the vocabulary.

4.5 DATASETS ARE NOT IN GIT
----------------------------
1.6 GB per 1000 games. Checkpoints are committed and datasets are not, so
on a second machine you can serve, play and measure - but retraining
needs a local recording.

======================================================================
5. WHEN TO STOP
======================================================================

Stop when a full cycle does not move the win rate.

Before concluding the loop is exhausted, check in this order:

  1. Was the server actually up? (net calls > 0 on the sweep rows)
  2. Was the greedy control near 50%?
  3. Is exploration the bottleneck? (see 4.1)
  4. Is POLICY_TOP_K too wide? Top-3 already held the right move 97.4% of
     the time. As agreement rises, narrower and deeper beats wider and
     shallower.

After that, the honest next moves are the ones in HANDOFF.md: the value
net as a leaf evaluator inside the search, more decks, and hidden
information - the search currently assumes the opponent's hand is known.

======================================================================
6. WORKING ON TWO MACHINES
======================================================================

Committed, so it travels:  checkpoints (*.pt, ~250 KB each), the
                           history JSONs, ai/training/vocabulary.json,
                           data/ai_stats.json
NOT committed:             ai/training/datasets/*.jsonl (1.6 GB/1000 games)

At the other machine:

    git pull
    cd ai/python && source .venv/bin/activate
    pip install -r requirements.txt        # first time only
    python -m mtgai.serve --policy-checkpoint checkpoints/policy_net.pt

Do not retrain and then pull, or you will be resolving a binary conflict
on a .pt file. Pull first, then train.
